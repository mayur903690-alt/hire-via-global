    Questions

    Recruitment FAQ

    How long does a hire usually take?

    It depends on the role, the market and how quickly interviews can be arranged. We agree a target timeline in the role brief and report against it.

    Can I see candidates before they are shortlisted?

    Yes. You can see the list at any stage. Shortlisting simply marks the candidates we recommend you interview.

    How is candidate information protected?

    Candidate details and CVs are shared only with the people involved in the hire, and are kept only as long as needed.

    Can you hire for several roles at once?

    Yes. Each role has its own brief and pipeline, so progress is tracked separately.
