KPI and Performance Management

Try the KPI calculator

Change any number to see how achievement is calculated. Achievement for a higher-is-better KPI is actual ÷ target × 100. For a lower-is-better KPI it is target ÷ actual × 100.

Status bands: 100 and above is target achieved, 85 to 99 on track, 60 to 84 needs attention, below 60 below target. A zero target on a higher-is-better KPI cannot be scored. On a lower-is-better KPI, an actual of 0 counts as 100.

Payroll and Reporting

Payroll inputs ready for review

We prepare salary structures, approved allowances, overtime and deductions for review and approval. Gross salary is basic salary plus allowances and eligible earnings. Net salary is gross salary minus authorized deductions.

Note: tax, social contribution and overtime rules depend on the country and your policies. They are applied only after those are confirmed. Hire Via Global prepares payroll data; it does not pay salaries.

  • Draft, review, approval and finalized stages
  • Payslips and payroll history
  • Headcount, attendance, leave and performance reports
  • CSV exports for finance teams
