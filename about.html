About Us

People operations that stay simple as you grow

Hire Via Global supports organizations with HR consulting, recruitment and staffing. We focus on clear processes, honest communication and results you can measure.

Our approach

Start with the requirement, then build the process

Most HR problems come from unclear expectations: a role that was never defined, a leave rule that nobody wrote down, a target that changed without notice. We begin by putting the requirement in writing, then design the simplest process that meets it.

From there we track what happens. Hiring stages, attendance, leave and performance are all recorded the same way every time, so managers can compare like with like and spot problems early.

Add before launch: company history, year founded, locations, team size and client names. These are left out until they are verified.

  • Requirement first

    We confirm what the role or process needs before any work begins.

  • Active communication

    You get regular updates and one point of contact.

  • Flexible engagement

    Use one service or several, and change the mix as your needs change.

  • Records you can rely on

    Every decision, approval and change is recorded so it can be reviewed later.

Working with us

What to expect

A first conversation

We listen to what you need, ask about your team and agree on what success looks like.

A written plan

You receive a short plan covering scope, responsibilities and timing before work starts.

Regular updates

Progress is shared on a schedule you choose, with open issues called out.

A review at the end

We compare results with the plan and recommend what to keep, change or add.

Principles

How we treat people and data

HR work involves personal information. We treat salary, documents and candidate details as confidential and share them only with people who need them for their role.

  • Access to records depends on role
  • Candidate and employee data is kept only as long as it is needed
  • Changes to sensitive records are logged
  • Legal and tax rules are applied only after your jurisdiction and policies are confirmed
